The Penalty Box: Your Game-Day Sanctuary.

Wall-to-wall screens ensure you never miss a cross-check or a buzzer-beater. Whether you’re serving two minutes for unsportsmanlike conduct or just hiding out from the real world, we’ve got a stool waiting with your name on it.

Signature Sports Cocktails

Puck Drop Punch (Hockey)

A refreshing power-play of vodka, blue curaçao, and grenadine layered with lemon-lime soda and dark cherry "puck" ice cubes. 
The Linebacker (Football)

A hard-hitting blend of high-proof bourbon, sweet vermouth, walnut bitters, and a smoke-infused maple syrup.
The Chalk Line (Baseball)

A smooth, heavy-hitting blend of aged bourbon, whole milk, heavy cream, and a sneaky "stolen" splash of coffee liqueur
Penalty Kick Punch (Soccer)

A vibrant, grass-green blend of tequila, Midori melon liqueur, and fresh lime juice, topped with a crisp club soda fizz and garnished with a mint sprig "sideline."
The Back Nine (Golf)

A vibrant, grass-green rally of tequila, sour apple liqueur, and fresh lime juice, topped with a crisp club soda fizz and a mint sprig "flagstick."
The Set Point (Tennis)

A vibrant, neon-yellow "ace" of white rum, limoncello, and fresh lemon juice, topped with a splash of lemon-lime soda.
